# Escalation and contact

## How to open a ticket

1. Sign in to the Contoso Cloud portal.
2. Go to *Support → New ticket*.
3. Pick a severity (S1 for production-down, S2 for degraded, S3 for
   how-to questions).
4. Provide a clear summary, the affected region, and a recent
   correlation ID from your application logs.

## Severity definitions

| Severity | Definition                                             | Eligible plans |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | -------------- |
| S1       | Production system is down or fully unavailable         | Enterprise     |
| S2       | Production is degraded but partially usable            | Pro, Enterprise |
| S3       | How-to or non-blocking question                        | All            |

## Channels by plan

- **Free** — community forum at *forum.contoso.cloud*.
- **Pro** — email *support@contoso.cloud*; in-portal chat 9 AM to
  6 PM in your region's local time.
- **Enterprise** — everything Pro has, plus 24/7 voice hotline and
  the dedicated TAM bridge described in the plan-tiers document.

## What support cannot do

Support engineers cannot make billing changes, refund invoices, or
modify contractual terms. For those, contact your account manager or
*billing@contoso.cloud*.
