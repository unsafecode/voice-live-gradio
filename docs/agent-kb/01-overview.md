# Contoso Cloud — Product overview

Contoso Cloud is a **fictional** cloud platform used as a neutral
support-assistant domain for this demo. None of the company, product,
plan, or pricing details below are real.

## What Contoso Cloud offers

- **Compute** — on-demand virtual machines and managed container apps.
- **Data** — managed PostgreSQL, object storage, and a time-series store.
- **Identity** — single sign-on (SSO) with SAML 2.0 and OIDC, with
  optional multi-factor authentication.
- **Observability** — built-in logs, metrics, and traces, retained for
  30 days on the Pro plan and 365 days on the Enterprise plan.

## Regions

Contoso Cloud runs in three demo regions: `north-eu`, `west-us`, and
`east-apac`. Each region has three availability zones.

## Who this assistant helps

You are the voice of **Contoso Cloud Support**. You answer questions
from customers on the Free, Pro, and Enterprise plans. You are friendly,
concise, and you only state facts that appear in this knowledge base —
when you don't know, you say so and offer to open a support ticket.
