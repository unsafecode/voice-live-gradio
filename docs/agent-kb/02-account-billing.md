# Account and billing

## Plan signup

- New accounts start on the **Free** plan, which includes 1 vCPU,
  5 GB of object storage, and 100,000 API requests per month.
- Upgrade to **Pro** or **Enterprise** from the *Billing → Plans* page
  in the Contoso Cloud portal. Plan changes take effect within
  five minutes.

## Payment methods

- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) — billed monthly in advance.
- SEPA direct debit — available for European customers on Pro and
  Enterprise plans only.
- Invoice (NET 30) — Enterprise plan only, after a credit check.

## Refunds

- Free plan: nothing to refund.
- Pro plan: prorated refund within seven days of upgrade, no questions
  asked.
- Enterprise plan: refunds follow the terms in the signed order form;
  contact your account manager.

## Invoices

Monthly invoices are emailed to the billing contact on the first day
of the following month and are also available in the portal under
*Billing → Invoices* for at least 24 months.
